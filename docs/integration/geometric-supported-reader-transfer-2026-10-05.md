## Causal interpretation and next decision

The loss sums target-token probability across occurrences. Correct tokens shared
by records can receive credit from the wrong record, unlike the factual source
winner. Training uses correct earlier answer tokens; generation uses its own
prefix. SourceEnd argmax is stopped, and this root-only fit gives Period/Stop no
direct adjoints. A finite gradient or lower teacher-forced CE cannot establish
correct record identity, ordered generation or completion.

A saved numerical source-view overlap audit, independently recomputed, finds
108/1,012 development payload token positions (10.67%) and 160/340 fresh positions
(47.06%) available in the competing record. Initial payload tokens overlap in
30/128 and 8/32 cases respectively. In eight fresh cases, every expected payload
token ID occurs somewhere in the competing record. These BPE token membership
counts include repeated positions and exclude Period/EOS. They do not establish
equal scores, actual wrong-source gradients, matching sequence order or an
indistinguishable full geometric state. They do establish that payload token
support alone need not require the requested record, motivating a separate
record-provenance credit discriminator rather than another unchanged dose.

The declared orders are complete. Weak and unstable source transfer calls for
offline unique-record ranking credit in the existing
geometric score adapters, with shared-token, label-swap and duplicate-alias
controls. Maximum summed Copy score per record matches the served factual route
but retains an extreme-value dependence on record length; a cue-only record score
is a distinct comparison. Do not add a runtime gold gate.

Keep source-correct terminal failures separate. Retained endpoint calibration is
a possible completion arm after honest rebinding to the updated root artifact.
The historical accepted Stop refinement repaired missed EOS, not early Stop or
missing Period; its CLI is on the unmerged [PR #1719](https://github.com/UOR-Foundation/uor-r4/pull/1719)
and is not present in this source tree. This is a delivery distinction, not evidence
of scientific retirement. Inspect actual endpoint
addresses and error phases before importing or repeating it. Among initially
correct rows that switch by their first error, most observed source switches in
the first order occur at the period boundary. This does not establish a general
need for persistent source state. During-payload switches motivate existing
source-allegiance credit. Wrong-token errors whose chosen token mass comes
predominantly from the expected record motivate existing prefix/occurrence credit;
a correct factual source winner alone does not establish token provenance.

After a mechanism is chosen using these fresh outputs, this panel is exposed
diagnostic evidence for that successor. Useful grounded conversation still requires
coherent learned compiler/store/reader/emitter binding and generated-history
evaluation. No general prose, reasoning, chat, geometric advantage, complete D11/D5
serving or energy qualification follows here.
