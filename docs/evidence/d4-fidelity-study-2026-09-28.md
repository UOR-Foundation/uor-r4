## 3. Mathematical Codec Specification

### 3.1. Incoherence Processing: Randomized Fast Walsh-Hadamard Transform (FWHT)
Weight matrices in neural language models exhibit heavy-tailed coordinate distributions with isolated large-magnitude outlier channels that dominate dyadic quantization error. We apply a randomized orthogonal transformation to diffuse outliers evenly across coordinates:
$$\tilde{W} = W \cdot H_d \cdot S$$
where:
- $H_d$ is the unnormalized Fast Walsh-Hadamard Transform of dimension $d = 2^k$ (computed via the $O(d \log_2 d)$ butterfly algorithm using integer addition and subtraction).
- $S = \text{diag}(s_1, \dots, s_d)$ is a deterministic sign modulation matrix where $s_i \in \{-1, +1\}$ generated by a Galois LFSR (seed 20260928).
- Multiplier-free normalization: row coordinates are scaled by $2^{-\lfloor \log_2(d)/2 \rfloor}$ via arithmetic right shifts.

### 3.2. Grouped 4-Bit Dyadic Codec (Arm 1: H+G4)
- **Grouping**: Each row of width $d$ is partitioned into non-overlapping groups of $G = 32$ coordinates.
- **Group Scale**: For group $g \in \{0, \dots, d/G - 1\}$, the maximum absolute amplitude is bounded by a dyadic power-of-two scale:
  $$s_g = 2^{\lceil \log_2(\max_{i \in g} |w_i| / 7.0) \rceil}$$
- **Integer Nibble Packing**: Values are quantized to signed 4-bit integers $q_i \in [-8, 7]$ and packed two nibbles per byte (low nibble first).
- **Multiplier-Free Dequantization**: Reconstitution at serving time evaluates:
  $$\hat{w}_i = q_i \cdot 2^{e_g} = q_i \ll e_g \quad (\text{or } q_i \gg -e_g)$$
  requiring zero multiplication operations.

### 3.3. Conway-Sloane $E_8$ Lattice Codec (Arm 2: H+E8)
- Coordinates are grouped into 8-dimensional vectors $x \in \mathbb{R}^8$.
- Projected onto the Gosset lattice $E_8 = D_8 \cup (D_8 + \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{1})$:
  - $D_8$ round: rounds coordinates to nearest integers, adjusting the coordinate with largest rounding error if $\sum \lfloor x_i \rceil \equiv 1 \pmod 2$.
  - Half-integer round: translates by $\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{1}$, rounds to $D_8$, translates back.
  - Chooses whichever lattice point minimizes Euclidean distance $\|x - \hat{x}\|_2^2$.
- The 240 minimal vectors of norm 2 (the roots of $E_8$) plus the zero origin are indexed in an 8-bit codebook, achieving an effective bit rate of $8 / 8 = 1.0$ to $2.125$ bits/coordinate.
