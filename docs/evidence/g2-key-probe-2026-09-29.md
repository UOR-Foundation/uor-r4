## Supporting diagnostic (`decode-1`, sealed)

A single linear probe per atom head on the *same* frozen features (30 000 full-batch steps) reaches train 0.994 / 1.000, held-out entity **0.963 / 0.994**, but held-out relation only **0.450 / 0.491**. So per-head atom information is linearly exposed, while the **joint key** and **held-out relation transfer** are not. That is why the guard fails at 24 k and at 1.5 M episode visits alike, and why further exposure was not attempted (progress-control rule).

## Design decisions recorded before the runs

- **Root bound amendment.** The pre-registered pairwise `|q·r| ≤ ½` for 14 relation roots is infeasible: an exhaustive search over the 120 icosians finds at most 12 mutually separated roots (the 24-cell `2T` antipodal quotient). The 14 relation roots are the best feasible greedy min-max subset at `RELATION_ROOT_MAX_INNER = φ/2 ≈ 0.809017`, indices `[0,2,4,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14,15,24,25]`; an in-source test records the obstruction. The entity head is a plain 8-way softmax in both arms; the compared factor is the relation-atom head.
- **Exposure amendment.** The first run (1 500 steps over 24 k episode visits) was diagnosed as exposure-limited; the predeclared fix cached only the supervision-row features (≈110 MiB RSS) and trained the four heads to convergence. No metric, guard or decision rule changed.
